DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. It measures a property&#x27;s ability to cover its mortgage payment with rental income. A DSCR of 1.0 means the rent exactly covers the mortgage; above 1.0 means positive cash flow. Can I get a DSCR loan with no income documentation? Yes! That&#x27;s one of the biggest advantages. DSCR loans qualify based on the property&#x27;s rental income, not your personal income. No tax returns, W-2s, or pay stubs are required. What DSCR ratio do I need to qualify? Most lenders require a minimum DSCR of 0.75–1.0. However, a DSCR of 1.25 or higher will typically qualify you for the best rates and terms. Can I finance multiple properties with DSCR loans? Absolutely. Unlike conventional loans which cap at 10 financed properties, DSCR loans have no limit on the number of investment properties you can finance.
