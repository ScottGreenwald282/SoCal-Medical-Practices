DSCR Direct is operated by Jennifer Kirby, a licensed mortgage broker (NMLS# 2672337) specializing exclusively in investor loans for real estate investors.
Wholesale Access
DSCR Direct operates through Loan Factory Inc (NMLS# 320841), one of the largest wholesale mortgage brokerage platforms in the country. That gives us the reach and leverage to place your loan with a wide pool of DSCR wholesale lenders and find the best pricing execution available in the market.
Exclusively DSCR
DSCR loans for real estate investors only. No generalist approach. Deep expertise in investor financing across the full spectrum of investor products: standard DSCR, fix and flip, ground-up construction, foreign national, ITIN, condotel, non-warrantable condo, blanket, cross-collateral, and BRRRR-strategy fix-to-rent.
Wholesale Pricing
Wholesale lender rates at a significant discount to retail bank pricing. Institutional pricing, no runaround. Every rate displayed on this site comes from the same wholesale pricing engines that licensed mortgage originators use when pricing loans for clients.
