As a top mortgage broker headquartered in Miami and serving all of Florida, Bennett Capital Partners specializes in securing financing for real estate investors seeking flexible loan options. Our
DSCR Loan Programs
are designed for property investors who want to qualify based on the cash flow generated by their investment properties, rather than relying on traditional income verification methods like W-2s or tax returns.
​
Whether you’re an experienced investor or just starting to build your portfolio, we help you qualify for a
DSCR mortgage
by focusing on your property's debt service coverage ratio (DSCR), ensuring that the rental income covers your mortgage payments.
​
With over 20 years of experience in the mortgage industry, we’ve mastered
DSCR loan requirements
and built strong relationships with a wide network of wholesale mortgage lenders and private lenders. This allows us to offer competitive rates and terms on
DSCR loans,
tailored to your unique investment strategy. We simplify the process, providing personalized guidance from start to finish, so you can focus on expanding your portfolio.
​
Our
DSCR Loan Programs
cater to investors looking to finance a variety of properties, including single-family homes, condos, multi-family units, and more. Lenders assess the income generated by your property to determine eligibility, making these loans ideal for those with complex financial situations or non-traditional income streams.
​
Whether you’re purchasing a new investment property, refinancing an existing one, or expanding your real estate portfolio, our experienced team is here to assist you every step of the way.

to learn more about how we can help you secure a mortgage tailored to your investment goals—whether you’re based in Miami or anywhere across Florida.
