Conventional Loan A flexible option with competitive rates for buyers and homeowners who qualify with stronger credit and stable income. Best for: Primary homes, second homes, and many refinances. FHA Loan An easier path to homeownership with more flexible credit guidelines and low down payment options. Best for: First-time buyers or buyers rebuilding credit. VA Loan Built for eligible Veterans and active-duty service members, with powerful benefits and often little to no down payment required. Best for: Military buyers looking for maximum affordability. USDA Loan A 0% down option for eligible properties in approved areas, with income limits that may apply. Best for: Buyers who want low upfront costs. Jumbo Loan For loan amounts above conforming limits—ideal for higher-priced homes with strong borrower profiles. Best for: High-balance purchases and refinances. Construction Loan Financing for building a new home or major renovation, then transitioning to long-term financing when complete. Best for: New builds and major renovations. Home Equity Loan Access the value you’ve built in your home for renovations, debt consolidation, or other goals—options may include a fixed-payment loan or a flexible line of credit. Best for: Home improvements, consolidating debt, or planned major expenses. ITIN Loan Home financing options for ITIN holders who may not qualify using traditional documentation. Best for: ITIN buyers purchasing or refinancing in Connecticut. Non-QM Loan Flexible options for self-employed or non-traditional income—like bank statements, P&amp;L, or asset-based qualifying. Best for: Self-employed or complex income. DSCR Loan Rental financing that can qualify using the property’s cash flow instead of personal income. Best for: Buy-and-hold investors. Hard Money Loan Fast, short-term financing for investor deals—often used for fix &amp; flip projects or a short-term hold before refinancing. Best for : Fix &amp; flip and bridge strategies. Commercial Loan Financing for commercial real estate and mixed-use properties with tailored terms. Best for: Business owners and investors buying income-producing property.
