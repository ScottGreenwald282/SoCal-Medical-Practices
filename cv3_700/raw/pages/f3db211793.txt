Engineer first. Mortgage broker by choice.
I didn’t grow up dreaming about mortgages. I grew up playing lacrosse, and that took me on a full-ride athletic scholarship to
Lawrence Technological University
in Southfield, Michigan, where I earned my
BS in Mechanical Engineering
. I worked the Baja SAE project, interned at
Ford Motor Company
for two years, then spent time at
FCA
in data analytics.
I was good at engineering. I just didn’t love it. What I loved was helping people figure out hard problems and walking away knowing it actually mattered to them. So I left corporate and joined
Rocket Mortgage
. Two years there, three more at
Swift Home Loans
as a senior broker, and now I’m at
West Capital Lending
as a Branch Manager — running my own shop, with access to 120+ wholesale lender partners.
The engineering training never went away. It just turned into something more useful:
I structure deals like puzzles.
What’s the cleanest path? What’s the right product for THIS borrower? Where can we save you money or time? That’s the work. And after thousands of closed loans, I’m pretty good at it.

How I actually work
I shop your deal across 120+ lenders.
Your file goes to whoever’s the best fit for YOUR situation — not whoever cuts me the easiest commission. That’s the entire point of working with a broker.
No rate quotes online. Ever.
Every borrower is different. Credit, property, occupancy, loan size, down payment, state — they all change the rate. Anyone quoting you online without those details is selling you, not helping you.
Win-win or no deal.
I like driving real value for real people. It feels good to make money while helping someone else make money too. If the deal doesn’t work for both of us, it’s not the right deal.
Engineering precision on every file.
I structure your loan like a problem to solve, not a product to sell. Where can we save you money? What’s the cleanest path to close? That’s the work.
