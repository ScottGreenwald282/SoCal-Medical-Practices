DSCR Loans for Real Estate Investors | Select DSCR Capital
Simple DSCR Financing for Serious Investors
DSCR loans for real estate investors using rental income, not personal income. Model purchases, refinances, and cash-out rental property scenarios.
Cash-flow based DSCR financing
Select DSCR Capital focuses on business-purpose DSCR financing for real estate investors purchasing, refinancing, or accessing equity in rental properties. The homepage introduces a property-income-based financing model where rental income, payment obligations, leverage, reserves, and investor execution matter more than traditional personal income documentation. It also gives investors a path to compare rental purchases, refinances, cash-out requests, short-term rental scenarios, and portfolio financing needs before requesting terms.
Interactive deal modeling
Investors can use the DSCR calculator preview to model purchase, refinance, and cash-out scenarios before requesting terms. The calculator is more than a simple DSCR ratio check because it helps estimate how rent, taxes, insurance, HOA dues, loan amount, interest rate, leverage, payment structure, and liquidity assumptions may affect DSCR, cash flow pressure, LTV, reserve needs, and the financing path a rental property may support.
Request DSCR terms
The intended next step is a structured review of the rental property scenario. Select DSCR Capital helps investors organize the key deal inputs, including property value, rent support, current debt, loan purpose, target leverage, reserves, entity ownership, and exit strategy, so the financing conversation starts with the numbers rather than guesswork. This helps investors prepare purchase, refinance, cash-out, and portfolio scenarios before requesting DSCR terms.
