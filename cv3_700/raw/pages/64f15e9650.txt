A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies the rental property based on whether projected rent covers the mortgage payment &mdash; typically 1.0 ratio minimum, with 20&ndash;25% down and 660+ FICO. Personal income is not used. &nbsp; The math is simple: a property&#39;s monthly rent (or projected rent for purchases) divided by the monthly PITIA payment equals its DSCR. A 1.0 DSCR means the property covers its own debt service. A 1.25 DSCR means rent exceeds debt service by 25% &mdash; the sweet spot for best pricing on most programs. &nbsp; For Arizona investors building portfolios &mdash; Sedona short-term rental properties, Flagstaff long-term cabins, Phoenix-corridor single-family rentals &mdash; DSCR loans solve the personal-DTI problem. You can scale a portfolio without each new acquisition triggering a personal-income underwrite. The lender appraises the property, the appraiser determines market rent (typically via Form 1007 rent schedule), the lender divides that rent by the proposed PITIA, and that ratio determines program eligibility and pricing tier. &nbsp; Step-by-step on a typical Arizona DSCR purchase: &nbsp; 1. Appraisal + rent schedule. Once under contract, the appraiser issues both a property value and a Form 1007 rent schedule. The 1007 establishes market rent the lender will use for DSCR calculation. &nbsp; 3. DSCR calculation. Monthly rent &divide; monthly PITIA (Principal + Interest + Taxes + Insurance + HOA, if applicable). Result must meet program minimum &mdash; typically 1.0, sometimes 0.75 on lower-LTV programs, 1.25+ for best pricing. &nbsp; 4. Underwriting.
