What is a DSCR Loan?
A Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) loan allows real estate investors to qualify for financing based primarily on a property’s rental income rather than their personal income.
Instead of reviewing tax returns, W-2s, or employment history, lenders evaluate whether the property generates enough income to cover the mortgage payment. This makes DSCR loans an ideal solution for investors who:
Own multiple properties
Write off significant expenses
Have complex or non-traditional income
Want a streamlined approval process
Why Use a DSCR Loan?
No personal income verification
Qualify based on the property’s cash flow, not your personal income. No tax returns, W-2s, or complex income calculations required.
Faster, simpler approvals
With fewer documentation requirements, DSCR loans offer a more streamlined process, helping you move from application to closing more efficiently.
Scale your portfolio with ease
As you acquire more properties, traditional financing can become restrictive. DSCR loans make it easier to continue growing without being limited by personal income caps.
Ideal for LLCs and seasoned investors
Designed with investors in mind, these loans are well-suited for purchases under an LLC and for borrowers with multiple properties or complex financial profiles.
