Mortgage Programs Available Through Indiana Lenders Greg Rank works with Indiana mortgage lenders who offer a full range of loan programs: Conventional Loans Conventional loans are the most common mortgage in Indiana. With a 620+ credit score and 3–20% down, conventional financing offers competitive rates and flexible terms from 10 to 30 years. Down payments as low as 3% are available for qualified first-time buyers. FHA Loans FHA loans are popular with Indiana first-time homebuyers because they require only 3.5% down and accept credit scores starting at 580. Indiana mortgage lenders offering FHA financing can help buyers who don’t have a large down payment or perfect credit. VA Loans Indiana is home to a large veteran population, and VA loans offer benefits few other programs match: no down payment required, no private mortgage insurance, and competitive rates. Greg is experienced with VA lending and works with Indiana lenders specializing in veteran financing. USDA Rural Housing Loans Large portions of Indiana qualify for USDA Rural Housing loans, which offer 100% financing (no down payment) for eligible properties in designated rural and suburban areas. If you’re buying in a smaller Indiana town or rural county, USDA may be worth a close look. Jumbo Loans For home purchases above the conforming loan limits, Greg works with Indiana jumbo lenders who offer competitive rates on luxury homes, high-value properties, and investment purchases exceeding $832,750 (the 2026 conforming limit, set annually by the Federal Housing Finance Agency). DSCR & Investor Loans Real estate investors in Indiana who want to qualify based on a property’s rental income rather than personal income can use DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loans. These are ideal for landlords growing a rental portfolio in Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, Evansville, or any other Indiana market.
