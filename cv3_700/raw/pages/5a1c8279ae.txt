Founded by Harry Hager, Rockhouse helps real estate investors, homebuyers, homeowners and self-employed borrowers evaluate mortgage options across multiple wholesale lenders. Harry works directly with clients and referral partners to structure financing around the borrower, property, income documentation and transaction—not around a single lender’s product menu. About Harry Hager Harry Hager is the founder and principal mortgage broker of Rockhouse Mortgage, LLC. He has more than a decade of mortgage-lending experience and approximately 15 years of prior banking experience. His approach is centered on identifying the approval path early, anticipating underwriting issues and matching each transaction with a lender whose guidelines fit the complete scenario. Why work with Rockhouse Mortgage? Multiple lender options As an independent mortgage brokerage, Rockhouse can compare programs from multiple wholesale lenders instead of limiting borrowers to one institution’s guidelines. Deal structuring Harry reviews important factors such as LTV, DSCR, DTI, credit, reserves, property eligibility, documentation and lender overlays before choosing an approval path. Direct access Clients and referral partners work directly with Harry throughout the mortgage process. Investor-friendly experience Rockhouse understands that rental properties, renovation projects, entity ownership, short-term rental income and unconventional properties require different underwriting strategies than standard owner-occupied loans. Location and service area Rockhouse Mortgage is based in Leesburg, Virginia and serves borrowers and real estate investors in states where Rockhouse Mortgage and Harry Hager are properly licensed or otherwise authorized to conduct business.
