It is a popular option for real estate investors who already own several properties, are self-employed, or want to buy through an LLC. How DSCR works The lender compares the property's expected monthly rent to its monthly housing payment (principal, interest, taxes, insurance and any HOA dues). If the rent covers the payment by the ratio the lender requires, the property may qualify, often without personal tax returns or pay stubs. Who DSCR loans are for Investors buying or refinancing long-term rental properties Investors buying short-term rentals, with some lenders accepting projected short-term rental income Owners of 2–4 family properties common in Greater Boston and the North Shore Borrowers who want to close in the name of an LLC Important to know DSCR loans are for investment properties only and are business-purpose loans; they cannot be used for a home you live in. Down payment, credit and reserve requirements vary by lender and by the property's cash flow. Rates are typically higher than owner-occupied conventional loans.
