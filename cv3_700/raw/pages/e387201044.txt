Strait / Mortgage Loan Officer I’m T.C. Strait, an Ohio mortgage loan officer based in Mason. NMLS 164070. I got into mortgage lending in 2002 after a friend talked me into a career change. I never left. About 10 years in, the company owner retired and gave me partial ownership. I’m now co-owner of a small, local community mortgage broker. Three of us total. Each operates independently. When you work with me, you work with me. Not a call center. Not a rotating team. Born and raised in Southwest Ohio. UC Bearcats season ticket holder. Business Economics degree from the University of Cincinnati, early 2000’s. Married. Two stepkids, 14 and 12. A four-year-old daughter we call The Ginger. Two dogs, both purebred idiot. What I Do Residential mortgages. Purchases and refinances. That’s it. I’m an independent broker, which means I shop your loan across 30+ wholesale lenders instead of being stuck with one bank’s product lineup. I compare relevant programs and pricing across those lenders based on each borrower’s circumstances. Loan types I originate: → Conventional → FHA → VA → USDA → Jumbo → DSCR → Non-QM / Bank Statement → Investment Property → Refinances (cash-out, divorce, debt consolidation) Licensed in Ohio. Core market: Mason , Maineville , Morrow , Loveland , and the rest of Southwest Ohio. What makes me different Underwriter-reviewed pre-approvals. Most pre-approvals are a loan officer running numbers through software and printing a letter. Mine aren’t. The actual underwriter who reviews your file when you go under contract has already signed off before you’ve found a house. Sellers and listing agents notice the difference. I answer. Nights, weekends, holidays. The questions don’t come up during business hours. They come up at 8pm when you’re lying in bed doing math in your head, or Sunday morning at an open house when the agent asks if you’re pre-approved. Hard cases are my specialty, not my problem. Self-employed buyers with write-offs. Sub-640 credit. Recent job changes. Non-traditional income. If I can’t get you approved today, I’ll build a real plan to get you there. One client took 8 months from credit coaching to closing table. That’s the job. Local. Really local. I know property taxes by county. I know which neighborhoods are still inside USDA boundaries. I know the closing attorneys in Warren, Hamilton, and Butler Counties by name. Community Sponsor of Little Miami School District events, PTO activities, and youth football. Also on my HOA board.
