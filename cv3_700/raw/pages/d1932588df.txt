Frequently Asked Questions
Can self-employed business owners get a mortgage without tax returns in Austin TX?
Yes. Self-employed Austin borrowers can qualify using
bank statement loans
(12 or 24 months of deposits),
1099-only programs
,
P&L loans
, or
asset depletion
— tax returns may not need to be the primary income document. These
non-QM programs
underwrite actual cash flow, not the income your write-offs reduced. Adam matches each borrower to the right wholesale lender across 40+ programs.
What is a DSCR loan and who qualifies in Texas?
A
DSCR loan
qualifies real estate investors on a property's rental income — not personal income. DSCR = gross rent ÷ PITIA. Most lenders prefer 1.0+, but no-ratio programs exist. Works for long-term, short-term (Airbnb/Vrbo), and small multifamily across Texas. Down payments start at 20–25%, no cap on properties owned, LLC vesting allowed.
How does asset depletion qualify high-net-worth borrowers in Texas?
Asset depletion
uses eligible assets to calculate monthly income for mortgage qualification. It may help retirees, business owners after a sale, or borrowers who need to supplement other income. The lender determines eligible accounts, required deductions and the calculation period. The result is qualifying income, not an approved loan amount.
What loan programs are available for complex-income borrowers in Austin TX?
Complex-income borrowers — self-employed owners,
K‑1 partners
, S‑corp owners,
investors
, foreign nationals, and HNW individuals — can use
bank statement
,
1099-only
,
P&L only
, asset depletion,
DSCR
, jumbo (above $832,750 in Travis County for 2026), and
one-time-close construction
. Adam compares 40+ wholesale lenders to maximize qualifying income per scenario.
How fast can a self-employed buyer close in Austin TX?
Most pre-approvals are issued within one business day once your file is complete. Bank statement, DSCR, and other non-QM loan timelines depend on program, property, appraisal, title, and documentation. Fast underwriting matters in Austin's purchase market — competitive offers often need rapid funding to win against cash buyers.
