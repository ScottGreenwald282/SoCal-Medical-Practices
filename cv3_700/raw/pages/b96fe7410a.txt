For a deeper dive, visit our complete DSCR FAQ . Get My DSCR Numbers What is a DSCR loan in Louisiana? A DSCR loan in Louisiana is an investment property mortgage that qualifies you on the rental property's cash flow rather than your personal income. The lender divides monthly rent by the monthly housing payment; 1.0 or above generally means the property supports the loan. It's available across every parish. Why is insurance such a problem for Louisiana DSCR loans? Because it sits inside the ratio and it's among the highest in the country. Carrier withdrawals after Laura and Ida pushed many properties into Louisiana Citizens, the insurer of last resort. Premiums vary widely by parish, roof age, and construction, so we quote before we underwrite. Do I need flood insurance on a Louisiana rental? If the property sits in a mapped high-risk flood zone and you're financing it, yes, lenders require it. Even outside those zones it's often worth carrying. FEMA's Risk Rating 2.0 prices flood property by property now, so premiums differ house to house rather than by broad zone. Does the Louisiana homestead exemption apply to my rental? No. The homestead exemption shelters a portion of assessed value for owner-occupied homes only. If you buy a rental from someone who lived in it, expect your tax bill to be higher than theirs. We model the non-exempt figure so the first bill isn't a surprise. Can I use a DSCR loan for a New Orleans short-term rental? It depends heavily on the property and the current ordinance. Orleans Parish regulates residential short-term rentals tightly, with permitting tied to owner occupancy in much of the city. If your ratio depends on STR income, we verify what the property can legally do before a lender relies on it. What credit score do I need for a DSCR loan in Louisiana? Most DSCR programs require around 660 to 680 minimum, with some going lower given a larger down payment or a stronger ratio. In Louisiana, though, the insurance figure usually constrains the deal well before your credit score does. How does Louisiana's civil law system affect my closing? Louisiana is the only civil law state in the country. Sales are executed as authentic acts before a notary with two witnesses rather than through a common-law style closing, and parishes serve the role counties do elsewhere. In practice it's routine, it just requires people who work here regularly. Can I close a Louisiana DSCR loan in an LLC? Yes, many DSCR lenders allow Louisiana properties to be titled in an LLC. Requirements around documentation, reserves, and personal guarantees vary by program. An out-of-state LLC will generally need to register with the Louisiana Secretary of State before closing.
