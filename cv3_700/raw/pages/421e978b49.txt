Craig Messman is the President of Molitor Financial Group and the Founder of The Messman Lending Team, both based in Chicago, Illinois. With over two decades in the mortgage industry, Craig has built a reputation that most loan officers only hear about in conversations, not experience firsthand. Craig entered the mortgage business in 2001, starting at a large bank where he learned what it actually takes to close loans under pressure. By 2005, he co-founded Molitor Financial Group alongside partner Troy Molitor, growing what started as a boutique operation into a firm with more than 50 professionals and a track record that speaks through its clients, not its marketing. What separates Craig from most mortgage brokers is how he thinks about a loan. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Quantitative Economics and Computer Science from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, and he applies that analytical foundation every time he structures a mortgage. He does not guess. He uses data, monitors market trends, and builds loan strategies around each borrower's specific financial picture. Whether you are a first-time homebuyer in the Chicago suburbs, a real estate investor evaluating a refinance, or a repeat buyer comparing conventional, FHA, VA, or USDA options, Craig approaches your situation with the same methodical intent he has refined over 20-plus years. As a licensed independent mortgage broker operating in Illinois, Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Indiana, Michigan, Ohio, Texas, and Wisconsin, Craig has access to a broad network of banks, credit unions, and wholesale lenders. That access means he shops the market on your behalf rather than locking you into one lender's products. The difference between working with a broker and walking into a retail bank is the difference between someone who works for you and someone who works for their employer's bottom line. The Messman Lending Team was built around a simple standard: every borrower deserves to understand exactly what they are getting into before they sign anything. That means clear explanations of loan terms, honest conversations about what you qualify for, and a team that answers the phone. Client reviews across platforms like Yelp and Redfin consistently point to the same thing, that working with Craig's team does not feel transactional. It feels like working with someone who is genuinely invested in the outcome. Beyond origination, Craig coaches and mentors loan officers inside Molitor Financial Group, helping newer professionals develop the skills and structure needed to build sustainable businesses in a competitive industry. He has been featured in Chicago Agent Magazine and on the AIME Broker to Broker podcast as one of Chicago's top mortgage producers. His approach to building internal systems, including a pod structure that places experienced processors at the front of the file, is widely cited by peers as a model for how a modern mortgage operation should run. If you are searching for a mortgage broker in Chicago, a home loan specialist in Illinois, or an independent lending team that prioritizes your financial outcome over a transaction count, Craig Messman and The Messman Lending Team are worth a direct conversation.
