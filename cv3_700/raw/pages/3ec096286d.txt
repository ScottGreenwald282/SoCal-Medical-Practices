Purchase loans with a complete file can move quickly, and refinances of owner-occupied homes include the standard 3-day TRID rescission period before funding. What is a DSCR loan and who is it for? A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan is a real estate investor mortgage that qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the subject property rather than the borrower's personal income or tax returns. The DSCR is calculated as gross monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA ; most lenders require 1.0 or higher. DSCR loans are ideal for landlords, short-term rental (Airbnb / VRBO) operators, and self-employed investors who want to grow a portfolio without conventional income documentation.
