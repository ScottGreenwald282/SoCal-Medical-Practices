Professional Background Omar Michel has 15+ years of experience in California mortgage origination, having worked with thousands of borrowers across San Diego County and Southern California. He began his career in mortgage banking before transitioning to the broker channel — where access to 100+ wholesale lenders allows him to find the most competitive rate and program for each borrower's specific situation rather than steering clients toward a single institution's products. Omar specializes in scenarios that major banks frequently decline: self-employed borrowers who qualify on bank deposits rather than tax returns, veterans using VA loan benefits at any price point, reverse mortgage candidates evaluating HECM and jumbo programs, real estate investors using DSCR qualification, and high-net-worth borrowers using asset depletion programs. He provides full bilingual English and Spanish mortgage service — from the initial consultation through closing — for San Diego's large bilingual community.
