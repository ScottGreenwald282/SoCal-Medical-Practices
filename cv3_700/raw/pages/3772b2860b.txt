Quick Answer: What Is a Florida DSCR Loan?
A Florida DSCR loan is an investment-property mortgage that qualifies you based on the rental income the property generates — not your personal tax returns, W-2s, or employment letters. Approval is built around whether the rent covers the housing payment. When projected or existing rent meets the program’s coverage ratio, you can apply online and move toward closing.
DSCR loans are particularly powerful in Florida because the state combines no income tax, massive tourism demand, year-round rental occupancy, and a deep foreign-investor pipeline. Alex Doce has been closing Florida investor loans since 1987 — whether the property is a Miami Beach condo, an Orlando short-term rental, a Tampa duplex, or a Florida Keys vacation home.

DSCR Loan Disadvantages
What to
Know Before
You Apply
DSCR loans aren’t the right fit for every situation. Four trade-offs to weigh against the qualification flexibility.
Slightly Higher Interest Rates
DSCR rates are typically higher than conventional mortgages — usually 5.50% to 8.00%, depending on credit, leverage, and coverage strength.
Larger Down Payment
Down payments generally start at 15%, though most programs require 20–25%. Stronger files unlock lower-down options.
Depends on Consistent Rent
Loan performance hinges on the property producing reliable rental income. Extended vacancy, hurricane-related disruption, or below-market rents can affect the long-term coverage you projected at closing.
Investment Properties Only
Florida DSCR loans cannot be used for a primary residence or a second home you intend to occupy — only rental properties.
Florida-specific note:
Hurricane and flood insurance are major line items in the PITI calculation, especially for coastal properties in Miami-Dade, Broward, the Keys, and Gulf-Coast markets. We model the full insurance bill upfront so the coverage ratio reflects real cash flow — not a sunny-day estimate.
How It Works
How Florida Investors
Secure a DSCR Loan
From property evaluation to closing, the path is the same whether you’re buying a Miami condo, an Orlando vacation rental, or a Tampa duplex.
Evaluate the property
Confirm the strategy — long-term workforce rental, seasonal short-term rental, or year-round vacation property. The strategy drives how income gets projected.
Gather rent comps
Pull lease comps for long-term properties in Tampa or Jacksonville. For STR markets like Orlando or the Keys, model peak-season projections from AirDNA-style data.
Calculate full PITI
Add principal, interest, property taxes, and insurance — including hurricane and flood coverage where applicable. Florida coastal insurance can’t be guessed; we pull real quotes.
Confirm coverage
Compare projected rent to the full PITI payment. If the ratio meets program guidelines — typically 1.0 or higher, with 0.75 and no-ratio options available — you’re ready to apply.
Apply online
The application takes about 12 minutes. No tax returns, W-2s, or employment letters required — ideal for foreign investors and out-of-state buyers entering Florida.
Submit documents
Send the smaller-than-usual document package to underwriting (full list below). Florida LLCs are common — have your entity docs ready.
Order the appraisal
A Florida-licensed appraiser verifies the property’s value and its market rent. Both numbers factor into final approval.
Close in 20–30 days
Sign final docs and fund. Most Florida DSCR loans close in 20 to 30 days from a complete file. We’ve done this thousands of times from Fort Lauderdale.

What is a DSCR loan program?
A DSCR loan program is designed for real estate investors who want to qualify based on property income rather than personal income. Instead of reviewing W-2s or tax returns, underwriting evaluates the property’s Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) to confirm rental income can cover the mortgage payment. The Doce Mortgage Group offers flexible DSCR programs — including no-ratio options — that help Florida investors scale portfolios without traditional income verification.
How does the DSCR loan work?
The DSCR loan works by comparing the property’s gross rental income to its PITI payment (principal, interest, taxes, and insurance). Most programs require a ratio of 1.0 or higher. At The Doce Mortgage Group, we offer programs with ratios as low as 0.75 — or no ratio at all — useful for vacant properties or rentals priced below market.

What are the DSCR loan requirements in Florida?
Florida DSCR loans typically require: a coverage ratio of 1.0 or higher (some programs allow 0.75 or no ratio), a FICO score of 300 or higher (680+ for best pricing), 20–25% down payment (some programs allow 15%), and an eligible income-producing property — single-family, 2–10 unit, condo (warrantable or non-warrantable), or short-term rental on Airbnb / VRBO.
Can I get a DSCR loan with no down payment?
Most DSCR programs require a down payment, typically starting at 15%. Fully zero-down DSCR loans aren’t available — lenders require borrower equity to manage investment-property risk. We can help you explore strategies that keep upfront costs lower and connect you with DSCR programs offering flexible terms.
