Why work with an independent broker
As a mortgage broker, I'm not limited to one bank's programs. My job is to compare rates, costs, and guidelines across the lenders I work with, then bring you the option that actually fits your situation: a first home, a move up, or a refinance.
That comparison takes a bit more upfront conversation than walking into a single branch, but it means the recommendation is built around your numbers, not one lender's product list.

Loan programs I offer
Every situation is different. Here are a few of the programs I work with most; the full list covers even more.
Conventional
A flexible option for buyers with steady credit and some down payment saved.
Learn more about conventional loans
FHA
Lower credit and down payment minimums, popular with first-time buyers.
Learn more about FHA loans
VA
A home loan benefit for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses.
Learn more about VA loans
USDA
Financing for eligible homes in qualifying rural and suburban areas.
Learn more about USDA loans
Jumbo
Financing for loan amounts above the conforming loan limit.
Learn more about jumbo loans
Renovation
Roll purchase and renovation costs into a single loan.
Learn more about renovation loans
DSCR
A non-QM option for real estate investors. Qualifying is based on the property's rental income rather than your personal income.
Learn more about DSCR loans
Bank Statement
A non-QM option for self-employed borrowers. Income is documented with bank statement deposits instead of tax returns.
Learn more about bank statement loans
Asset Depletion
A non-QM option for borrowers with substantial savings or investments. Qualifying income is calculated from your assets rather than a paycheck.
Learn more about asset depletion loans
