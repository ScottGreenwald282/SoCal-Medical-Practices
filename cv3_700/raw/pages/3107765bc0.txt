Written by Travis Erickson&nbsp; &nbsp;Branch Manager &amp; Licensed Mortgage Loan Officer&nbsp; &nbsp;NMLS#1193479 &nbsp; Bonelli Financial Group Last updated: May 2026 ﻿ What Is a DSCR Loan? A Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan qualifies you based on the income a rental property generates &mdash; not your personal W-2, tax returns, or debt-to-income ratio. Lenders look at one number: does the property's monthly rent cover the mortgage payment? DSCR = Gross Monthly Rent &divide; Monthly Mortgage Payment (PITIA) A DSCR of 1.0 means the rent exactly covers the payment. Most lenders approve at 1.0 or above. Some go as low as 0.75 with a larger down payment. Example: A Mesa rental brings in $2,800/month. The mortgage PITIA is $2,200/month. DSCR = 1.27 &mdash; approved with strong terms. Frequently Asked Questions About DSCR Loan &nbsp;Can I get a DSCR loan if I'm self-employed? Yes &mdash; that's exactly what DSCR loans are designed for. Because qualification is based on the property's rental income rather than your personal income, your tax write-offs and business structure don't factor into approval. Do DSCR loans work for Airbnb or short-term rentals? Yes. Most lenders accept short-term rental income using a market rent analysis (Form 1007 or equivalent). We work with lenders who specialize in STR markets across Arizona, Florida, Colorado, and South Carolina. What's the minimum DSCR to qualify? Most lenders require 1.0 (break-even). Some allow as low as 0.75 with a larger down payment or stronger credit. We work with lenders across the full range &mdash; if your deal makes sense, there's usually a path. Can I purchase in an LLC? Yes. Most DSCR programs allow LLC or entity titling. This is one of the main reasons investors prefer DSCR &mdash; you can keep your portfolio in LLCs for liability protection without triggering conventional due-on-sale issues. How is a DSCR loan different from a bank statement loan? A bank statement loan qualifies you based on your personal or business deposits. A DSCR loan qualifies you based solely on the property's rental income &mdash; your deposits don't matter. For investment properties with solid rent, DSCR is almost always the faster and cleaner option. How fast can a DSCR loan close? Typically 21&ndash;35 days. With minimal income documentation to underwrite, the main variable is appraisal turnaround &mdash; which averages 10&ndash;14 days in most Arizona markets. What credit score do I need? Most programs start at 620. You'll get meaningfully better rates at 680+ and the best pricing at 700+. A lower score doesn't automatically disqualify you &mdash; it just affects your rate tier and may require a slightly larger down payment. Can I use a DSCR loan to refinance? Yes. Cash-out refinances are one of the most common uses. Investors pull equity from existing rentals to fund the next acquisition &mdash; all without income documentation. Rate-and-term refinances are also available. Is there a limit on how many DSCR loans I can have? No &mdash; unlike conventional financing , DSCR loans don't cap the number of financed properties. This makes them the standard vehicle for investors scaling beyond four to ten properties. Do you lend in my state?
