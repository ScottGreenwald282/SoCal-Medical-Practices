How Non-QM Loans Differ From Conventional and FHA Loans Conventional and FHA loans follow standardized Qualified Mortgage (QM) guidelines. These programs typically require full income documentation, tax returns, strict debt-to-income (DTI) limits, and agency underwriting standards. Non-QM loans provide greater flexibility in several key areas: Alternative income documentation such as bank statements , P&amp;L statements , or asset depletion Qualification based on rental income through DSCR rather than personal DTI Flexible debt-to-income calculations Interest-only options in certain portfolio programs Expanded guidelines for unique property types and investment scenarios While interest rates on Non-QM loans may differ from agency products depending on risk profile, these programs offer access to financing solutions that traditional underwriting models cannot accommodate.
