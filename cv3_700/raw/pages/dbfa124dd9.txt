DSCR loans qualify on the property's rental income, not on your personal DTI or tax returns. We work with multiple DSCR-focused wholesale investors and shop the file across the panel. Typical lock at 1.0 DSCR, 75% LTV, 30-year fixed. More on DSCR loans , or run your numbers on the calculator . What's the difference between a conventional investment loan and DSCR? Conventional uses your W-2 and tax returns to qualify and caps at ten financed properties. DSCR uses the property's projected rental income, has no DTI requirement, and has no financed-property cap with most investors. If you're past four financed properties or your W-2 income is inconsistent, DSCR is usually the smarter play. Can I lock my rate while I shop? Yes.
