Why Choose DSCR Loans?
DSCR loans offer unique advantages for real estate investors looking to expand their portfolio quickly and efficiently.
No Income Verification
Qualify based on property cash flow, not personal income. Perfect for self-employed investors.
Streamlined Process
Streamlined documentation and processing through authorized wholesale lender partners. Timelines depend on the wholesale lender, property, and borrower file.
Multiple Properties
Finance investment properties subject to wholesale lender program eligibility. Loan Daddy LLC is a broker, not a lender.
Program Flexibility
Access a range of DSCR programs from authorized wholesale lender partners, including terms up to 30 years where eligible. Rates and terms are set by the lender and subject to change.
