Hawaii real estate follows its own rules. From time zones and recording cutoffs to unique land title and escrow procedures, Hawaii lending process is unique. Mainland lenders unfamiliar with these details can cause funding delays, missed recording deadlines, or even last-minute loan denials. Working with a local Hawaii lender means your loan is handled by local mortgage experts. Do I need to be in Hawaii to work with your team? Not at all. Many of our clients live on the mainland or overseas but are purchasing or refinancing property in Hawaii. Our secure digital platform makes it easy to apply, upload documents, and close on time. No matter where you are in the world, you can get qualified and close your mortgage all remotely. Can I still qualify for a home loan if I’m self-employed? Yes! We work with many business owners and offer flexible income documentation options that go beyond traditional tax returns. Whether you use bank statements, CPA-prepared financial, DSCR loans, or other income methods, we’ll help structure a loan that fits your financial profile. How long does it take to close a loan in Hawaii? Most loans close within 15–30 days, depending on the loan type and property location. Because we coordinate directly with Hawaii-based escrow and title companies, our local relationships can often help speed up the process. Do I have to put 20% down to buy a home? Not at all. We offer a variety of flexible, low down payment options for conventional and first-time homebuyer loans, as well as zero-down payment programs for VA and USDA eligible borrowers. How long does it take to close a home loan? On average, most loans close in 21–30 days, depending on the program and how quickly documents are provided. Our team keeps you updated at every step. Do you offer VA loans? Yes, and some of our loan officers are Veterans themselves. We know the VA loan process inside and out and are committed to helping you make the most of your benefits. Because we work with multiple lenders, we compare options to help you find competitive rates and terms tailored to your financial goals. What affects my mortgage rate? Rates depend on several factors such as credit score, loan type, down payment, and even property location. We&#x27;ll evaluate your financial profile and help you explore the competitive rate options available to you. Can I buy an investment property through your company? Absolutely. We offer flexible financing for both primary and investment homes, including programs that consider rental income. What’s the first step in getting per-approved? It starts with a conversation with one of our licensed loan officers. We&#x27;ll review your financial goals, credit history, and income to determine your eligibility for a pre-approval letter to share with your real estate agent. Do I need to find a Realtor before talking to a lender? It’s best to start with the lender first. Getting per-approved helps you understand how much you can afford, strengthens your offer, and gives your Realtor a clear budget to work with. Once you know your financing options, your Realtor can help you focus on homes that fit your price range. What’s the difference between a local or national bank and a mortgage broker like your company? Think of working with a bank like shopping at a single-brand store. They only offer their own products and prices. Working with a mortgage broker gives you access to a wide network of lenders and loan programs in one place. As an independent broker, we review options across our network of 60+ wholesale lending partners to help you find a competitive loan program that fits your needs. Is this a good time to get a mortgage? There’s no perfect time. Rates fluctuate, but waiting for the “perfect” rate can mean missing out on home appreciation or the property you really want. We can help you evaluate the numbers to see if now aligns with your financial goals and long-term plans. Can I add my children to the mortgage? You can add children or family members as co-borrowers if they meet income and credit qualifications. Keep in mind, this also means they’ll share financial responsibility for the loan. If you’re adding them to the title only (not the loan), it must be handled carefully after closing to avoid triggering loan or ownership issues. What’s the minimum credit score to qualify for a mortgage? It depends on the loan program. Conventional loans typically require a score of 620+, while FHA and VA loans may allow lower scores with additional factors like strong income or down payment. We’ll help review your credit and recommend the best loan options for your profile. How can I complete the entire loan process remotely? Our process is fully digital from start to finish. You can apply online, securely upload documents, and sign most forms electronically. We communicate through phone, email, and video when needed. We’ll be keeping you updated every step of the way.
