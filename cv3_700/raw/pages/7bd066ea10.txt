How do I qualify for a mortgage in Missouri? Core Underwriting Factors Lenders evaluate a full financial profile to measure borrowing capacity and loan safety, assessing credit history, gross monthly income, debt-to-income (DTI) ratio, employment stability, liquid cash reserves, and down payment savings. Requirements vary significantly based on the property type, occupancy plans, and transaction scope, whether financing a primary single-family home, a multi-family property, an investment home, or commercial real estate. W-2 & Salaried Employees Qualification relies on standard documentation, such as recent pay stubs, W-2 forms, and tax returns, to establish predictable, recurring earnings. Self-Employed & Alternative Income Borrowers Specialized financing solutions evaluate income structure through non-traditional documentation, including 12-to-24-month personal or business bank statements, profit and loss (P&L) statements, or asset depletion models, to align with real personal and business cash flows. Getting pre-approved upfront helps you know your true borrowing power and establishes a clear budget before you start your home search or make offers. What credit score is needed to qualify for a mortgage in Missouri? Credit score expectations depend on the loan program: Government-Backed Loans (FHA, VA, USDA): FHA and VA programs allow credit scores starting as low as 580 (or lower with a larger down payment). USDA loans generally look for a 640 score. Conventional Loans: Most lenders prefer scores of 620 or higher. Reaching 740+ unlocks lower interest rates and lower private mortgage insurance (PMI) premiums. State Assistance Programs (MHDC): Programs backed by the Missouri Housing Development Commission typically require a minimum credit score of 640. Non-QM & Jumbo Loans: Guidelines vary based on liquid reserves, income documentation type, down payment size, and specific wholesale lender criteria. DSCR Investor Loans: Minimum credit scores range from 600 to 700+, depending on down payment size and property rental cash flow. How much down payment is required to buy a home in Missouri? Down payment requirements vary based on loan program, occupancy, and borrower profile: VA Loans: 0% down payment required for eligible military service members, veterans, and surviving spouses. USDA Loans: 0% down payment required for qualifying properties in designated rural zones. Conventional Loans (Primary Residence): As little as 3% down for qualified first-time buyers, and 5% down for standard primary home purchases. FHA Loans: Minimum 3.5% down payment for primary residences. First-Time Homebuyer & Assistance Programs: Low down payment options (3% to 3.5%) that can be paired with Missouri Housing Development Commission assistance options. Second Homes & Vacation Properties: Typically require 10% to 15% or more down. DSCR & Investment Property Loans: Typically require 15% to 20%+ down based on property cash flow and investor guidelines. Are there first-time homebuyer programs in Missouri? Yes. The Missouri Housing Development Commission (MHDC) offers specialized programs to assist eligible first-time and repeat homebuyers across the state: MHDC First Place Program: Designed for first-time buyers and eligible veterans, offering competitive fixed interest rates combined with down payment and closing cost assistance. MHDC Next Step Program: Available to both first-time and repeat buyers seeking competitive financing paired with down payment and closing cost assistance options. MHDC Cash Assistance Development (DPA): Provides a second mortgage equal to 4% or 5% of the total loan amount to cover upfront closing expenses and down payment requirements. Eligibility Criteria: Applicants must meet program income and purchase price limits, meet a minimum 640 credit score requirement, and occupy the home as their primary residence.
