Texas DSCR Loan FAQs
How DSCR Loans Work for Texas Real Estate Investors
At Crestmark Lending, our Texas DSCR loan programs are designed for investors who want to qualify based on property performance—not personal income. No tax returns, W-2s, or traditional DTI requirements.
We compare the property’s rental income to the full monthly payment (PITIA) to calculate the Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR). If the income supports the payment, the deal can qualify.
This streamlined approach helps investors move faster, scale their portfolios, and keep financing focused on the asset—not personal finances.
What credit score and DSCR ratio are required to qualify?
In
Texas
, most DSCR lenders require a minimum credit score of 660 and a DSCR ratio of 1.00 or higher, meaning the rental income covers the monthly payment. Some Texas lenders may allow lower DSCR ratios—including below 1.0—with stronger credit, higher reserves, or pricing adjustments.
How much do I need for a down payment on a DSCR loan?
Down payment requirements for DSCR loans typically range from 20% to 25%, depending on credit, property type, and the loan program. In Texas, stronger borrower profiles may qualify for higher leverage options. By working with a mortgage broker, you gain access to multiple DSCR lenders—helping you lock in more competitive terms for your investment.
Can I close a DSCR loan in an LLC?
Yes. In Texas, DSCR loans are designed specifically for real estate investors and can be closed in an LLC, corporation, or other business entity. This structure helps investors separate liability, maintain clean bookkeeping, and manage multiple rental properties under a single entity.
