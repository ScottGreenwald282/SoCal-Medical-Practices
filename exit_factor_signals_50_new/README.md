# 50 new Greater Des Moines owner signals

This folder is a signal file. It is not another owner directory. The frozen baseline is `baseline_excluded.tsv`: the 63 companies already delivered in `exit_factor_500/priority_100.csv` and `owner_ready.csv`, plus company names already used in `exit_factor_250/`. Those names, aliases, DBAs, parents, and duplicate locations are not new.

An accepted row needs a verified current full-name owner and one source-backed operating event from the last 12 months, or up to 24 months when the event is still relevant. There is no revenue requirement. A street address is not required when the local base is otherwise credible. A missing direct email or mobile does not disqualify a row.

Events that count: dated expansion, new facility, new branch, or new geography; an acquisition by the owner-led business; documented succession or a next-generation handoff; appointment of a GM, COO, or a new management layer; reported growth, capacity, or fleet investment; or the owner discussing margin, labor, or operating-system challenges. Founder age, years in business, reviews, generic hiring, family ownership, existing multiple locations, a routine completed job, and a service list are not signals. Hiring counts only when it is tied to a documented expansion or management change. Nothing here says an owner intends to sell, is in distress, or is retiring.

`qualified_signals.csv` is the accepted file, one strongest current signal per company. `review_required.csv` and `excluded.csv` keep the rejects. `source_log.csv` records searches. Research date: 2026-10-01.
